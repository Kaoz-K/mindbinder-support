## Why MindBinder?

- **Capture everything** — rich notes with headings, checklists, callouts and
  collapsible sections; Markdown, slash commands, inline images and powerful
  search. Organise with projects, nested tags and inline #tags.
- **Tasks & reminders** — multiple to-do lists per project, due-date and
  recurring reminders, plus interactive home-screen widgets on iPhone, iPad
  and Mac.
- **Write your book** — book projects with chapters, character sheets,
  world-building entries and writing statistics (word counts, streaks, goals).
- **Private by design** — local-first storage, optional iCloud sync in *your
  own* iCloud, automatic backups, optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock. No
  accounts, no tracking, no ads.
- **Export & import** — PDF, ePub, Word (.docx), HTML and Markdown.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is my writing stored?**
Locally on your device. With iCloud Sync enabled, it also lives in your own
iCloud Drive so your Mac, iPhone and iPad stay in sync. The developer never
has access to your writing.

**How do I enable sync?**
Settings → Sync → enable iCloud Sync on each device, signed in to the same
Apple ID.

**How do I export my book?**
Open a project and use the export action to create PDF, ePub, Word (.docx),
HTML or Markdown files.

**I lost data — can I get it back?**
The app keeps automatic local backups. Go to Settings → Backups to restore a
recent snapshot.

**How does the AI assistant work?**
It is optional and off by default. Connect your own Anthropic API key in
Settings to enable it; selected text is then sent directly to Anthropic only
when you use the assistant.

**How does MindBinder Pro work?**
The core app is free. MindBinder Pro is an optional subscription
(€2.99/month or €29.99/year, with a 1-month free trial) that unlocks the full
writing toolkit. Manage or cancel anytime via your App Store subscriptions.
