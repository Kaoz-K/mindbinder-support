# MindBinder — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 July 2026_

MindBinder is a notes and writing app for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It was built to
keep your notes and writing private and under your own control.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** MindBinder has no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no
advertising. We (the developer) never receive, store or process your notes,
manuscripts or any other personal data.

## Where your data lives

- Everything you write is stored **locally on your device** in the app's own
  database.
- If you enable **iCloud Sync**, your database is stored in **your own iCloud
  Drive** (Apple's CloudDocuments service) so your devices can stay in sync.
  This data is only accessible through your Apple ID; we have no access to it.
  Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in
  [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- The app keeps automatic **local backups** of your database on your device so
  your work is recoverable.

## Optional AI assistant

MindBinder contains an optional AI writing assistant. It is **off by default**
and only works if you connect your **own Anthropic API key**. When you actively
use it, the text you select is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's
API to generate a response. We never see this data, and nothing is sent
anywhere unless you trigger it yourself. Anthropic's handling of API data is
described in [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

## Notifications

Reminders you set are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data
leaves your device.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to hand over,
correct or delete: your data is yours, on your devices. Deleting the app (and,
if you used iCloud Sync, the MindBinder folder in your iCloud Drive) removes
all of it.
